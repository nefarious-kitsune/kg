Event Rewards

In this event, Lords have a chance to obtain the following rewards.

  • Alex
  • Lighthouse Watch Frame
  • Polar Adventure Castle
  • Snowfield Guardian Castle

Event Format

Snowfield First Aid

Collect Energy Stones by completing daily quests:

  • Defeat Titan and monsters
  • Pass stages
  • Gather gold
Pinball Legend

Consume Energy Stones to play a marble game. The score from each round is based on the points from the box where the marble land. The marbles may also collide with the Barricade during their descent, and after 5 collisions, the Lord can earn extra points displayed by the Barricade.

Road to Rescue

Consume game points earned from the mini-game to unlock Event Pass rewards for Medical Kit:

  • Free-tier: 500 Medical Kit
  • Paid-tier: 1350 Medical Kit and a permanent Avatar Frame
Wheel of Fate

Consume Medical Kit (collected from Event Pass, Quests, Wishing Pool, and event pack purchases) to draw rewards from the event treasure vault. Tap the upper-right button to switch between the Normal Mode and the Crazy Mode.

Wishing Spring

Consume Gems to make a wish at the Wishing Pool and receive rewards. The first 100 wishes made daily will grant a Medical Kit.

Half of all gems used will be added to the grand prize, and one lucky Lord will be randomly selected to win the grand prize.
