Overview

Evil Spirit Battlefield is a weekly alliance battle event that lasts for 30 minutes.

The event is divided into three phases: registration, battle, and display.

Players earn points by defeating monsters and bosses to improve personal and alliance ranking.

Registration

To register for the event, the alliance must meet the following requirements:

  • More than 10 active players within 7 days before the event starts
  • Overall power must reach over 100,000,000

R5/R4 may register the Alliance for the event by choosing from one of 12 possible time slots: UTC 0100, UTC 0300, UTC 0400,… UTC 2300.

Each alliance can register up to 2 time slots. Time slots cannot be changed after registration.

Points and Leaderboards

In each time slot, the Alliance can earn Alliance Points by defeating bosses. If two time slots are registered, points from both time slots will add up for ranking in Server and Cross-Server Leaderboards.

Entering the Battlefield

Each alliance may enter the Battlefield during the registered time slot(s). Each member can participate in 1 time slot each week.

When a member enters the Battlefield, the member will get 1 free Teleport. The member will get additional free Teleports at the 25:00, 23:00, and 21:00 marks.
