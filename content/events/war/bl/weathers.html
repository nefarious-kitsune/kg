The Weather System is currently under beta testing. Please refer to the official announcement and in-game text for the latest updates.

During Burning Expedition, each day may have one of 5 types of weathers.

Schedule

Mon. Tue. Wed. Thu. Fri. Sat. Sun.

Weathers of day 1, 12, and 21 are Clear Day

Weathers of day 6, 13, and 18 are influenced by votes.

Weathers of other days will be Sandstorm, Thundercloud, Snowy, or Rainy.

Clear Day

  • No bonus

Sandstorm

  • +10% bonus power for Terra Faction
  • +50% bonus power for Arwyn, Arthur, Sahar, Padme, Eleanora, Green
  • +20% Proportion of Slightly Wounded

Thundercloud

  • +10% bonus power for Inferno Faction
  • +50% bonus power for Ophelia, Miku, Erika, Tracy, Kirona
    • Alex
  • +20% Proportion of Slightly Wounded

Snowy Day

  • +10% bonus power for Frost Faction
  • +50% bonus power for Merlin, Hadi, Jessica, Clarence, Olaf, Hathes
  • +20% Proportion of Slightly Wounded

Rainy Day

  • +10% bonus power for Verdant Faction
  • +50% bonus power for Alucard, Claudia, Gruen, Tumnus, Webster, Viola
  • +20% Proportion of Slightly Wounded

Voting

  • Each player with Crystal Season Card can vote twice per day.
  • Each player without Crystal Season Card can vote once per day.
  • On the day that players vote the weather on, the probability of a specific weather depends on the number of votes for that weather.

Weather Heroes

Power boost from the Weather Heroes apply to the entire Troop March, whether the hero is the leader or an assistant. Power boost from Weather Heroes can stack.
