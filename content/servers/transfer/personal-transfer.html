Important things to remember before/after you transfer.

  • If your destination server requires application, send a transfer request at least two days before Free Server Transfer opens so you do not have to wait for 2 days.
  • Claim alliance gold before you leave your alliance or transfer.
  • Dimensional Tunnel resets after transfer (your troops automatically get removed from Dimensional Tunnel mines). Send your troops back to the mines after transfer as soon as possible.

Resets

Certain progresses get reset after transfer.

Alliance Resets
Alliances Quests Complete as few Alliance Quests as possible. If you have any quest completed, collect the rewards before transfer; if you do not, the quests will be marked as completed but you will not get any rewards from it.
Lava Trial Rewards limits on Lava Trial reset. Before you transfer, summon all available Haka Behemoth and join as many Haka Behemoth rallies as you can.
Evil Spirit Invasion If you complete Evil Spirit Invasion before transfer, you will receive Kingdom ranking rewards after you transfer to the new server.
Lava Cave Lava Cave resets. Use all your points and collect all rewards before you transfer; you will not be able to participate in Lava Cave for the remainder of the week.
