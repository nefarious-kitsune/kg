Hunters (AP Heroes)

Hunter Heroes (AP Heroes) are ideal for doing quest because of their AP discount and damage immunity abilities.

Action Point Discount

Hunter Heroes can reduce Action Point cost needed for monster quest and scavenging quest:

  • For attacking a monster, Dark Priest, or Frost Behemoth: 109 APs
  • For starting a rally on a Titan, Evil Guard, or Frost Behemoth: 2018 APs
  • For collecting a Scavenger Chest: 109 APs
  • For attacking a Dragon Nest for first-occupation reward: ? APs

Damage Recovery

Hunter Heroes can recover damage from battle against certain monsters after returning to City:

  • Titans
  • Lava Behemoth and Lava Haka
  • Evil Defenders (unoccupied Fortresses/Dragon Nests)
  • Monsters, ES Titans, and ES King in ES Battlefield
  • Attack Lane and Transport Lane monsters
  • Frost Behemoth
