A castle has maximum 5 bar of health, and it will lose one bar after each failed defense.

  • What happens when a castle's health drops below zero?
  • How fast does the castle heal?
  • Where do ported castle go?
  • What if the player does not log back in?

What happens when a castle's health drops below zero?

If a castle's health drops to zero (0) and is attacked again, the castle will be removed from the map (ported).

How fast does the castle heal?

Attacked castle will recover 1 bar of health per hour.

Where do ported castle go?

If a castle is removed from the map, the castle will appear in a random location when the player logs back in.

What if the player does not log back in?

If a castle is ported and the player does not log back in, the castle will not appear in the map. The player will not be removed form the Kingdom or the alliance because of it, however.
