Overview

First, let's begin with an analogy. Let's say you are a healthy adult with no special medical condition.

  • If you get a paper cut, it is normally a slight wound, and you may go on with everyday routines while your body heals itself.
  • If you have a broken bone, it is a major injury that requires urgent medical attention.

In the game, when Troop March receives battle damage, some portion of the damage is considered Slight Wound and the Troop March will recover that portion automatically.

Sources

Sources of Proportion of Slightly Wounded are

  • Days of Sandstorm, Thundercloud, Snowy, or Rainy Weathers (+20%)
  • Days of Goddess of War (+5% or +10%)
  • Wound Regeneration skill (+10% or +15%).

Regeneration vs First Aid

Some heroes (e.g. Enzo) have bonus Recovery Speed from the First Aid skill.

Some heroes (e.g. Clarence) have bonus Proportion of Slightly Wounded from the Wound Regeneration skill.

So, which one is better?

In general, Wound Regeneration is preferred because:

  • If a Troop March with Wound Regeneration skill is reinforcing a friendly castle or stationing at a war building, the Troop March can regain power after combats. That is equivalently a power bonus.

  • If a Troop March with Wound Regeneration skill is stationing at a war building, all other Troop Marches at the building shares that skill and also regain some power after combats.

  • If a Troop March is reinforcing a friendly castle, and the Rally Captain has Wound Regeneration skill, the reinforcement Troop March shares that skill and also regain some power after combats.
