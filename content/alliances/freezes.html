During war events, participating Alliances are frozen and Lords cannot join or leave a frozen Alliance.

If an alliance successfully registers for Burning Expedition, the alliance will be frozen at UTC 0000 on Saturday, and members will receive a notification mail.

On the Cross-Server Invasion day, all alliances are frozen for 24 hours

If an alliance successfully registers for Battle of the Dragon (Dragon Valley Showdown), the alliance will be frozen.

If an alliance registers for Evil Spirit Battlefield, the alliance will not be frozen because of it.
