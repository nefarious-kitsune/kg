    March 1

    Kris Ophelia

    Gold Master

    Builder

    For gold gathering and building obelisks. Kris has bonus March Speed and Gathering Speed. These two heroes get more exp-level upgrades so that the Troops March has more carrying load and can stay in a Dragon Nest longer.

    March 2

    Harold Arwyn

    Hunter

    Stage Master

    For starting Titan rallies. Harold provides Action-Point discount, so he can help do more monster rallies. Harold can also increase attacks of all units in Tower Defense.
