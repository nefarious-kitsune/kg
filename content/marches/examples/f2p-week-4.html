    • Example build for a F2P player after 4 weeks of playing. — by Kitsune21

    March 1

    Kris Ophelia Samar

    Gold Master

    For gold gathering. Kris has bonus March Speed and Gathering Speed. These three heroes get more exp-level upgrades so that the Troops March has more carrying load and can stay in a Dragon Nest longer.

    March 2

    Harold Arwyn

    Hunter

    Stage Master

    For starting Titan rallies. Harold provides Action-Point discount, so he can help do more monster rallies. Harold can also increase attacks of all units in Tower Defense.

    March 3

    Alucard Gabriel

    Gold Master

    For gold gathering. Alucard can increase offline gold output.
