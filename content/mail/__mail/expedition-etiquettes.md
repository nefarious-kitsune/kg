<size=60><b><color=teal>Expedition Etiquettes</color></b></size>

When you are in Broken Land, please follow these etiquettes:

☆ We do not attack our allies. We do not get any event points when we attack our allies. (Exception: if our ally is stuck with a long march and ask for help, we can destroy their castle so their troops will be home immediately )

☆ Be courteous to other people. Don't start drama because that will make it difficult for the leaders to make allies.

☆ If you see a marching line going toward a gold mine, find another gold mine.

☆ Do not abandon a gold mine unless it's absolutely necessary.

☆ Finish your own Evil Guards as soon as you can. Do not leave a free Evil Guard out there.

☆ Do not steal Evil Guard. If you did not defeat the Dark Priests, the Evil Guard is not yours.

☆ Do not try to be helpful and defeat the Dark Priests for other people. That will cause confusion and lead to stealing.
