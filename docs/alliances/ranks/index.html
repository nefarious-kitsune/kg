Alliance Member Ranks

R5 & R4 Members

R5 and R4 are Alliance leaders and they have broad permission.

R1–4 Members

In many alliances, all active, non-leadership members are R3. Members who break alliance rule or are inactive are assigned to R2 or R1 as a warning. Some alliances assign all alt accounts to the R2 for tracking purpose.
