Alliance Member Ranks

R5 is the ultimate leader of the alliance.

Permissions

R5 has broad permissions within the alliance.

  • Transfer alliance to a different server
  • Change alliance tag/name
  • Change member application settings
  • Approve/deny member applications
  • Promote member to R4 & demote members from R4
  • Promote member to R2–R3 & demote members from R2–R3
  • Edit alliance notice message
  • Send alliance mail
  • Construct/delete Central/Expedition Fortresses
  • Construct/delete obelisks
  • Delete Dragon Nest
  • Set/remove alliance marks on maps
  • Recall troops from obelisks/Dragon Nests
  • Summon Lava Haka
  • Start Evil Spirit Invasion
  • Make/edit Cross-Server Summon post
  • Approve/deny Cross-Server Summon applicants
  • Set/remove flags in Lava Cave
  • Register alliance events
  • Distribute event gifts

Leadership Transfer

The R5 can transfer leadership to any member. When leadership is transferred, the ranks will swap. For example, if the R5 transfer leadership to a R3 member, the former leader will become an R3.

If the R5 is inactive for extended period of time, the R5 can be removed from leadership via impeachment.

An R4 can impeach when the R5 is inactive for 3 days or longer. An R1–R3 can impeach when the R5 is inactive for 5 days or longer.
