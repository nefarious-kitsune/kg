    Titles & Roles

    Generally R4 members lead war efforts. However, not all R4 are fighters; they may be entrusted to the role because they are trustworthy and knowledgeable about the game. To avoid confusion, R5 may appoint R4 to the appropriate positions:

    Title Roles
    Mentor Provide guidance on events and distribute event rewards
    Architect Plan and build Alliance territories
    Diplomat Communicate with other Alliances on pacts
    God/Goddess of War Execute battle plans
    Commander Set and execute battle plans

    Permissions

    R4 has broad powers within the alliance.

    Power Permission
    Transfer alliance to a different server
    Change alliance tag/name
    Change member application settings
    Approve/deny member applications
    Promote member to R4 & demote members from R4
    Promote member to R2–R3 & demote members from R2–R3
    Edit alliance notice message
    Send alliance mail
    Construct/delete Central/Expedition Fortresses (Architect only)
    Construct/delete obelisks (Architect only)
    Delete Dragon Nest (Architect only)
    Set/remove alliance marks on maps
    Recall troops from obelisks/Dragon Nests
    Summon Lava Haka
    Start Evil Spirit Invasion
    Make/edit Cross-Server Summon post
    Approve/deny Cross-Server Summon applicants
    Set/remove flags in Lava Cave
    Register alliance events
    Distribute event gifts (Mentor-only)
