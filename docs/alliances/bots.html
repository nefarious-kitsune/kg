Bots

If an alliance is set to Everyone can join and has spaces for more members, bot accounts will randomly appear on the map. Bots can help alliance members fill Titan rallies and bots will also start Titan rallies. Bots always have the same power and are always under shields.

Adding/Removing Bots

While bots can help alliance members do their daily quests, bots can also a nuisance during Evil Spirit Invasion or Lava Haka summons because they can pull troops away from castles with their rallies.

To remove bots, set your alliance's Join Restriction to Application required, then manually remove the bots from the alliance.

To get the bots back, set your alliance's Join Restriction back to Everyone can join.

Spawning Locations

The Spawning locations of the bots are random and cannot be controlled. If the alliance has a Central Fortress, bots will always appear in that vicinity.
