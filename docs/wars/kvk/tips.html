Tips for Burning Expedition

Teleports

Every day, you get 4 free teleports to move between Normal Land and Broken Land. Use those free teleports wisely.

  • When the alliance moves hive, use a free teleport to return to Normal Land and then another one to move to the new hive on Broken Land.
  • When doing Element Lord, use the free teleports to port close to the Element Lord.
  • Before the start of the Cross-Server Invasion event, use the free teleports to port to the Scorched Land.

First Occupation

Buildings such as Fortresses and Moutain Passes are initially guarded by high-power monsters. Use AP-discount heroes (e.g. Miku, Harold, Pedra, Jessica, and Pan) to defeat the monsters. (Attacking buildings do not require Action Points, but those heroes can recover damage from attacking monsters).

Auto Rally

Change Auto Rally settings after every teleport. Titans in Broken Land are Lv. 16 and you need to change your Auto Rally settings to join Lv. 16 Titan rallies. If not, you won't be able to join Titan rallies.

Communication

R4/R5 should send regular instruction and reminder mail on what to do on Broken Land.

Events

Events such as Evil Spirit Invasion and Lava Haka should happen in Broken Land only. (The developer is making adjustment and Evil Spirit Invasion is currently unavailable on Broken Land).

To enter Rushing to Dragon Valley, players must return to Normal Land first before they can enter the event.
