Bots

Answers to frequently asked questions on bot accounts

  • What are bots?
  • How do I remove bots?
  • Can I move bots to Expedition Fortress?

What are bots?

If your alliance is set to Everyone can join, then bot members may join your alliance and randomly appear in your Central Fortress. The bot members will help fill Titan rallies and also start rallies. Bot members always have the same power and are always under shields.

How do I remove bots?

Bots can be a nuisance during Evil Spirit Invasion or Lava Haka summons because they can pull troops away from castles with their Titan rallies.

To remove bots, set your alliance to Application required, then remove the bots.

To add bots back, set your alliance to Everyone can join, and the bots should join momentarily.

Can I move bots to Expedition Fortress?

Unfortunately, if you have a Central Fortress, bots will always appear there.
