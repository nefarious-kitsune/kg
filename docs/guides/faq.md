## Bots

### What are bot accounts?

If your alliance is set to "Everyone can join", then bot accounts will randomly appear
in your Central Fortress. The accounts will help fill Titan rallies and also start
rallies. The accounts always have the same power and are always under shields.

### How do I remove bot accounts?

Bot accounts are great to help your members do their daily quests. But they are also
a nuisance during Evil Spirit Invasion or Lava Haka summons because they can pull troops
away from castles with their rallies.

To *remove bots*, set your alliance to "Application required", then remove the bots.

To *get the bots back*, set your alliance to back "Everyone can join".

### Can I get bots to appear in our Expedition Fortress?

Unfortunately, if you have a Central Fortress, bots will always appear there.

## Goddess Trial

### How do I pass Tech levels?

Do Tech upgrades in Academy. Tech Power in Goddess Trial is related to the
number of upgrades in the Academy.
