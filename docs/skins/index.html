Avatar Frame, Castle Skin, and Scene

You can increase your troops power by using Castle Skins, Avatar Frames, and Scenes (unlocked after castle reaches Lv. 500).

Skins and Frames are either Permanent or Time-Limited.

Permanent Skins

If you own a Permanent skin, your troops receive a power bonus from the Owned Stats.

If you equip the Permanent skin, your troops receive an additional power bonus from the Use Stats.

See usage examples.

Time-Limited Skins

If you have a Time-Limited skin and it isn't in use (it's in your bag) your troops do not receives any power bonus from the skin.

If you use a Time-Limited skin (take it out from your bag) your troops receive a power bonus from the Owned Stats for a limited time.

If you equip the Time-Limited skin, your troops receive an additional power bonus from the Use Stats for a limited time.

See usage examples.
