Phase 1

The first phase of the server starts with the Great Fog and ends with the Sentry War.

See Phase 1 Calendar

The Great Fog

The Great Fog that segregate players into different zones. Players cannot move outside of their zone or join alliances in a different zone. The Fog will gradually lift and allow for movement.

Tip: If you tap on the Fog, the game will show you when that section of the Fog will unlock for you.

  • On Day 1~6, the map is divided into 8 different zones. (Map)
  • On Day 7, the Fog partially lifts and the 8 zones merge into 4 zones. (Map)
  • On Day 11, the Fog partially lifts and the 4 zones merge into 2 zones. (Map)
  • On Day 16, the Fog completely lifts and the whole map is revealed.

Welcome Events

In the first phase, the server will have several welcome events. The welcome events do not repeat.

  • Imperial Treasure I on the first 7-days of a new player account. Players can obtain free VIP Points through this event.
  • Crazy Harold on the first 7-days of a new player account. Players can obtain Harold through this event.
  • First Mightiest Lord on Day 3 ~ 9. Players can obtain Filius through this event.
  • Easter Adventure on Day 7 ~ 14. Players can obtain Avatar Frame, Castle Skin, and Bella through this event.
  • First Personal Championship on Day 10 ~ 16. Players can obtain Ptolemy through this event.
  • First King of Power on Day 17 ~ 20. Players can obtain time-limited Castle Skin through this event.
  • Titan Expedition on Day 18 ~ 22. Players can obtain Avatar Frame through this event.
  • Second Mightiest Lord on Day 21 ~ 27. Players can obtain Pedra through this event.
  • First in-server Auction House on Day 15 ~ 18. Players can bid against other players in the server for Padme, Tracy, Clarence, and Tumnus.
