VIP Perk Notes

Notes on power bonuses from VIP perks

  • Increase power of all garrisoned troops: the power bonus applies to all troops garrisoning inside the player's own castle. The power bonus does not applies to reinforcement troops from allies.
  • Archer Power: the power bonus applies to all Archer troops (heroes and units).
  • Flame Mage Power: the power bonus applies to all Flame Mage troops (heroes and units).
  • Ice Wizard Power: the power bonus applies to all Ice Wizard troops (heroes and units).
  • Goblin Power: the power bonus applies to all Goblin troops (heroes and units).
  • All Troops Power: the power bonus applies to all troops (heroes and units).
