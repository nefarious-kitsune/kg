Alliance Transfer

Transfer Process

  • Only the R5 can initiate the Alliance Transfer process.
  • Once the Transfer is initiated, the alliance cannot add any new member.
  • After the Alliance Transfer is initiated, each member has 24 hours to accept or decline the transfer. Only members who accept the transfer will be transferred. Members who decline the transfer or do not accept the transfer within 24 hours will be removed from the alliance.

Resets

Certain progresses get reset after transfer.

Dimensional Tunnel All members who transfer with the alliance will have their troops automatically get removed from Dimensional Tunnel mines after you transfer. Send a reminder mail to ask members to send troops back to the mines as soon as possible.
Evil Spirit Invasion Evil Spirit Invasion resets and the alliance needs to start from level 1.
Alliance Tech Alliance Tech do not reset.
