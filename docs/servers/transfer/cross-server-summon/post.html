Posting Cross-Server Summon

On the last week of each KvK season, there is a 5-Day Alliance Cross-Server Summon event. During the event, alliances can post a Beckon to recruit players from other servers.

An alliance can post a summon if all of the followings are satisfied:

  • The alliance has at least 10 open spots

  • The alliance has more than 30 active members in the last 30 days

  • The alliance has not applied for server transfer.

Quotas

Each alliance has a certain quota depending on the member count.

Member Count Quota
< 4030
40~6910
≥ 705

Posting

  • An R5/R4 of an eligible alliance can post a Beckon.

  • When posting a Beckon, the R4/R5 can select Alliance Tag, Activity Time, and Minimum Power

  • Alliance Tag states if the alliance is for fighting (Conquer the World), casual play (Active Growth), or farming (Peaceful Development)

  • Activity Time states when the alliance normally does its events: UTC 0100 ~ 0700, UTC 0700 ~ 1300, UTC 1300 ~ 1900, or UTC 1900 ~ 0100

  • After a Beckon is posted, it can be modified but not cancelled.

Reviewing

R4 and R5 can review application and choose to accept or deny players. When a player is accepted, the player is immediately transferred to the alliance.
