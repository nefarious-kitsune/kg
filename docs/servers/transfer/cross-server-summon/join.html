Joining Cross-Server Summon

At the end of each season, there is a 6-Day Alliance Cross-Server Summon event. During the event, players can join alliances from other servers by applying to summons.

Eligibility

  • The player must have at least Lv 500 Castle.

  • The player must not belong to an alliance.

  • The player is not under cooldown from an alliance transfer.

Joining a Cross-Server Rally

  • A player can respond to a Cross-Server Summon and apply for transfer (Rally). A player can have maximum 3 Rallies (applications) at a time.

  • The R5 or R4 of the summoning alliance will review the application and choose to either accept or deny the player.

  • If the application is approved, the player immediately and automatically transfers to the alliance.
