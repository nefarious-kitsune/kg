Joining Cross-Server Summon

On the last week of each KvK season, there is a 5-Day Alliance Cross-Server Summon event. During the event, players can join alliances from other servers by applying to Alliance Beckons.

A player can respond to an Alliance Beckon if all of the followings are satisfied:

  • The player has at least Lv 500 Castle.

  • The player does not belong to an alliance.

  • The player is not under cooldown from an alliance transfer.

Applying

  • A player can respond to a posted Beckon and apply for transfer (Rally). A player can have maximum 3 transfer applications (Rallies) at any given time.

  • An R5 or R4 of the summoning alliance can review the application and either approve or deny the application.

  • If the application is approved, the player immediately transfers to the alliance.

  • If the application is denied, the player cannot re-apply to the same alliance.

Tips

  • You have to leave your current alliance before you can respond to a Beckon. Make sure you claim Alliance gold before you leave.

  • When you transfer, some progresses will rest, and there are several things that you should do before and after transfer.

  • Check the status of the server on the Server Transfer listings. If the server is listed as Not active enough in the Can't transfer tab, the server is probably temporarily locked and may be disbanded soon.
