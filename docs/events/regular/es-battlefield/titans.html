ES-Battlefield Titan and Evil Spirit King

  • Appearances
  • Titans
  • King

On the battlefield, there are 4 Titans and one Evil Spirit King.

Order of Appearances

  • Level 1 Element Titan appear by default

  • Level 1 Evil Spirit King appears when all 4 Level-1 Titans are killed

  • Level 2–10 Titan appear when its previous level is killed

  • Level 2–9 Evil Spirit King appear when its previous level is killed

  • Level 10 Evil Spirit King appear when

    • All 4 Level-10 Titans are killed
    • Level-9 Evil Spirit King is killed

Elemental Titans

There are 4 Element Titans. Each can only be damaged by a specific counter element.

Titan Location Counter Element

Level 2–10, 12–20 Element Titans resurrect 30 seconds after its previous level is killed.
