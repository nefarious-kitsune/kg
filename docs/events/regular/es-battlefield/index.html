Overview

Evil Spirit Battlefield is a weekly alliance battle event that lasts for 30 minutes.

The event is divided into three phases: registration, battle, and display.

Players earn points by defeating monsters and bosses to improve personal and alliance ranking.

Requirements

  • More than 10 active players within 7 days before the event starts
  • Overall power must reach over 100,000,000

Event Details

  • Battlefield Boss: Titans
  • Battlefield Boss: Evil Spirit King
  • Monsters and Evil Spirit Items
  • Leaderboards
