Monsters & Evil Spirit Chests

  • Searching

    • In the Evil Spirit Battlefield event, Lords can attack monsters for Evil Spirit Chests.

    Searching for Monsters

    Monsters are not visible at the start of the event. To see the monsters, Lords need to use the Search Map function. Tier-1 Orc Warrior unlocks at the start of the event. For the first 4 minutes, a higher tier monster unlocks every minute.

    Monsters and Chests

    When the Lord defeats a monster on the Evil Spirit Battlefield, the Lord can obtain an Evil Spirit Chest. The Lord needs to return the chest to the castle to get rewards.

    Monster Evil Spirit Chest
    Orc Warrior T1 Chest
    • 100 points
    • 1× Custom Construction Material
    • Alliance Supply (10% chance)
    Goblin Priest T2 Chest
    • 200 points
    • 2× Custom Construction Material
    • Alliance Inspiration (10% chance)
    Cyclops T3 Chest
    • 400 points
    • 3× Custom Construction Material
    • Counter Holy Sword (10% chance)
    Lava Behemoth T4 Chest
    • 800 points
    • 3× Custom Construction Material
    • Suppressing Holy Water (10% chance)
    Flame Demon T5 Chest
    • 1600 points
    • 5× Custom Construction Material
    • Heavenly Fire (10% chance)

    Notes on Chests

    • Carrying an Evil Spirit Chest will increase the Troop March's movement speed (+50% for each Chest).
    • Rewards will only be settled when the Evil Spirit Chest is brought back to the castle.
    • Each troop can carry one Evil Spirit Chest. If a Troop March defeat multiple monsters, the Evil Spirit Points will add up and the Chest will be upgraded.
