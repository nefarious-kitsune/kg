Evil Spirit Invasion

Evil Spirit Invasion is a regular event that happens on Monday and Thursday every week.

  • Mechanics of Evil Spirit Invasion
  • Tips for all players
  • Tips for alliance leaders

Rewards

In ESI, alliance members can earn:

  • Alliance Silver (max 21,000)
  • Dragon Soul Stone (max 400)
  • Normal Chest (max 16)
  • Advanced Chest (max 4)
  • Blueprints (max 20)
    • Pre-Season: T4 Blueprint (Immortal Armor)
    • Season 1–6: T4 Blueprint (Immortal Armor))
    • Season 7+: T5 Blueprint (Legendary Armor)

Alliance members can also earn Advanced Chest, Deluxe Dragon Soul Stone, and Dragon Soul Stone from in-server and cross-server rankings.
