Tips on the Evil Spirit Invasion event (for alliance leaders)

Start Time

ESI should always starts at a fixed time. Your active members need do a lot of prep work and changing the event time can be disruptive. Make sure that you have multiple R4/R5 available at the scheduled times.

If you can, schedule the event after UTC 1300 (5 PM at developer's time zone). It's unlikely that the developer would run a server maintenance after that time.

## Evil Spirit Invasion Tips (for all players) ### How reinforcement works When you reinforce the Fortress, everyone gets the same power bonus as the biggest troops. Similarly, when you reinforce a castle, you may also get some power bonus. So, if you are small, do as much reinforcement as you can. But if you are big, do as little as possible. ### How to get points if you are small - If you need reinforcement, share your location in Alliance Chat and ask for help. - If you are done with Wave-19 Normal Spirits, start reinforcing. - If your castle has :x::x: mark, it means you will no longer get any attack from Normal Spirits. So start reinforcing. - Reinforce castles targeted by Elite Spirits first. - Reinforcing the Fortress at the end. ### Over-reinforcement If a castle does not need reinforcement, and you reinforce the castle with a big Troop March that is substantially bigger than the castle's total power, you may __steal points__ and the member being reinforced could get __0__ point for the entire event. When you reinforce others, it is best to - Stay online and reinforce at the right timing, - Send a Troop March with appropriate power (power down your Troop March if necessary)

Mail & Communication

Communication is a key to having a successful run of the event. Your Alliance Notice should note the event times for Evil Spirit Invasion (and Lava Haka). Regularly send out reminder mail on the time and rules of the event.

Alliance Rules

To maximize event points, the following alliance rules are recommended:

No unnecessary reinforcement
Check the event tab for castles that need reinforcement (the alert box will be highlighted in red). If you reinforce a castle that doesn't reinforcement, you are stealing points from your fellow alliance members. If your reinforcing troop is substantially larger than the castle, your fellow alliance member may get no point for the event.
No teleport during Evil Spirit Invasion
If you teleport for any reason, your castle will get two ❌ marks.
Stay close to the hive
The window for reinforcement is only 5 minutes (less if you need to recall troops afterward). If member castles are too spread out, then reinforcement would not be possible.
No rally on Titan or Evil Guard during Evil Spirit Invasion
If you start a rally, you can pull troops away from your fellow members' castles and may cause them to have a ❌ mark.
Big player: reinforce the Alliance Fortress with your weakest troop (and one troop only)
If you use a weak troop, you can boost strength of small players and maximize their points.
Small player: no limit on reinforcing the Alliance Fortress

If your alliance has 80+ members, the following additional rule is recommended:

Maximum one troops in the Dragon Nest closest to the alliance hive
You want all member castles to be around the hive. If the Dragon Nest is full, then members who have slow-marching troops may be forced to teleport to an available Dragon Nest.

Bots

If your alliance rely on bots, it is recommended that you remove bots no link before the events and add them back later.
