Tips on the Evil Spirit Invasion event (for all players)

Maximizing Points

Stay close to hive
With each attack wave, alliances members only have 5 minutes to react and send reinforcement. Stay close to everyone else so all needed reinforcement are within a marching distance.
Check the event notification
Check the event notifications. If a castle needs reinforcement, the notification box will be highlighted in red. (see ESI notification screenshot)
Teleport with caution

If your castle is not being targeted by any Evil Spirit, you may teleport your castle to reinforce others. Your castle may momentarily receive ❌❌ mark, but the mark should reset to the previous state on the next attack wave.

However, if your castle is being targeted (an Evil Spirit is marching toward you) and you teleport, your castle will receive ❌❌ mark and you will no longer get any Evil Spirit.

If your castle has ❌❌ mark…
If your castle has ❌❌ mark, it means you will no longer get any attack from Evil Spirits. So do as much reinforcement as you can.
Do not over-reinforce

If you reinforce a member with a Troop March that is substantially bigger than his/her total power, you may steal points and the member being reinforced could get no point.

When you reinforce others, send a Trop March with appropriate power. Power down your Troop March if necessary.
