Winter Surprise

Event Rewards

In this event, Lords have a chance to obtain the following rewards.

  • Christine
  • Winter Reindeer Dream Frame
  • Candy Snow Hat Castle
  • Snowman Cookie Dream Castle

Event Format

Gingerbread Adventure

Collect Christmas Hooks by completing daily quests:

  • Defeat Titan and monsters
  • Pass stages
  • Gather gold
Gift Selection

Consume Christmas Hooks to play a gift selection game.

Winter Points Pass

Consume game points earned from the mini-game to unlock Event Pass rewards for Christmas Stockings:

  • Free-tier: 500 Christmas Stockings
  • Paid-tier: 1350 Christmas Stockings and a permanent Avatar Frame
Surprise Spin and Win

Consume Christmas Stockings (collected from Event Pass, Quests, Wishing Pool, and event pack purchases) to draw rewards from the event treasure vault. Tap the upper-right button to switch between the Normal Mode and the Crazy Mode.

Christmas Hearth

Consume Gems to make a wish at the Wishing Pool and receive rewards. The first 100 wishes made daily will grant a Christmas Stockings.

Half of all gems used will be added to the grand prize, and one lucky Lord will be randomly selected to win the grand prize.
