Gunpowder Lab

Event Rewards

In this event, Lords have a chance to obtain the following rewards.

  • Lovelace
  • Inventor Frame
  • Robot Rabbit Assistant Castle
  • Robot Rabbit Doctor Castle

Event Format

Material Claim Experiment

Claim gift for Gunpowder Pack during gift distribution times (UTC 0100, UTC 0700, UTC 1300, and UTC 1900). Each Lord may claim the gift twice each day.

Collect Chest

Search for chest locations and dispatch troops to collect chests. For every 4 chests collected, receive an experimental item.

Gunpowder Research

Consume experiment item earned from Collect Chest to unlock Event Pass rewards for Gunpowder Packs:

  • Free-tier: 500 Gunpowder Packs
  • Paid-tier: 1350 Gunpowder Packs and 1 permanent Avatar Frame
Gunpowder Grinder

Consume Gems to make a wish at the Wishing Pool and receive rewards. The first 100 wishes made daily will grant a Gunpowder Packs.

Half of all gems used will be added to the grand prize, and one lucky Lord will be randomly selected to win the grand prize.

Gunpowder Experiment

Consume Gunpowder Packs (collected from Event Pass, gifts, Wishing Pool, and event pack purchases) to draw rewards from the event treasure vault. Tap the upper-right button to switch between the Normal Mode and the Crazy Mode.
