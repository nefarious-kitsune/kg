Burning Expedition

Burning Expedition is a 3-week event in the Seasonal Phase

Overview

Burning Expedition is a 3-week event in the Seasonal Phase.

The event happens on the Broken Land and is a battle between four Factions. Each faction may consist of one or more factions.

Event Days

Event registration is Monday–Friday on Week 4 of each season.

The event happens on Week 1–3 of each season. The Throne War happens on Sunday of Week 3.

Registration

To register for the event, the alliance must meet the following requirements:

  • The number of members who logged into the Alliance in the past 30 days is not less than 30
  • Alliance Power reaches 400,000,000

Alliances that meet the requirements are automatically registered (unless if the registration is cancelled by an R5/R4 member).

If an alliance successfully registers for Burning Expedition, the alliance will be frozen at UTC 0000 on Saturday, and members will receive a notification mail. If the alliance fails to register, members will not receive any notification.
