Chapter 37:

Missions

  • Earn 100 daily quest point(s)
  • Perform Hero Recruitment 10 time(s)
  • Upgrade any 1 Hero(es) to Lv. 70
  • Power reaches 55,000,000
  • Castle reaches Lv. 215

Rewards

  • 410 Gem
  • 10 Epic Dragon Rune
  • 100 Stone
  • 500K Gold

Chapter 38:

Missions

  • Challenge any level of monsters 5 time(s)
  • Upgrade Any Unit 20 time(s)
  • Upgrade any 2 Hero(es) to Lv. 70
  • Power reaches 60,000,000
  • Castle reaches Lv. 220

Rewards

  • 420 Gem
  • 40 Advanced Recruitment
  • 100 Stone
  • 500K Gold

Chapter 39:

Missions

  • Challenge any level of monsters 10 time(s)
  • Perform Hero Recruitment 10 time(s)
  • Upgrade 1 Dragon Emblem(s) to Lv. 160
  • Power reaches 70,000,000
  • Castle reaches Lv. 230

Rewards

  • 430 Gem
  • 50 Advanced Recruitment
  • 100 Stone
  • 500K Gold

Chapter 40: Top Priority

Missions

  • Challenge any level of monsters 10 time(s)
  • Upgrade 1 Dragon Emblem(s) to Lv. 200
  • Upgrade any 1 Hero(es) to Lv. 80
  • Power reaches 80,000,000
  • Castle reaches Lv. 240

Rewards

  • 440 Gem
  • 60 Advanced Recruitment
  • 100 Stone
