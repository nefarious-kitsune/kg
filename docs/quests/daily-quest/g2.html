G2 & G3 Daily Quest

On G2 Server Group and G3 Server Group, Daily Quest unlocks after Castle reaches Lv. 8.

  • Each day, gain 100 quest points to unlock Daily rewards
  • Each week, accumulate 700 quest points to unlock Weekly rewards

Daily Rewards

Rewards for 20, 40, 60, and 80 quest points are the same as those from G4 Daily Quest.

20 Quest Points

  • 2 Skill Shard
  • 3 Normal Recruitment
  • 1 10 AP
  • 100 Daniel

40 Quest Points

  • 3 Skill Shard
  • 3 Normal Recruitment
  • 1 10 AP
  • 100 Daniel

60 Quest Points

  • 3 Skill Shard
  • 3 Normal Recruitment
  • 1 10 AP
  • 100 Daniel

80 Quest Points

  • 3 Skill Shard
  • 3 Normal Recruitment
  • 1 10 AP
  • 100 Daniel

100 Quest Points

  • 500 Gem
  • 5 Advanced Recruitment
  • 3 Skill Shard
  • 1 Stats Recruitment
  • 300 Daniel

Weekly Rewards

Weekly Rewards are different from those from G2 Daily Quest.

700 Quest Points

  • 500 Gem
  • 10 Advanced Recruitment
  • 1 Advanced Teleport
  • 1 8-Hour Shield
