    Light and Dark Magic do not need to be balanced.

    Light Magic Leveling

    The Light Magic Level is enhanced by consuming Light Reagent. For every Light Reagent consumed, the Light Magic Power increases by 1400. The maximum Light Magic Level is 2000 and requires 1000000 Light Regent.

    See Light Magic Leveling.

    Light Magic Power

    Each Hero in a Troops March has a Light Magic Power based on the Light Magic Level. The maximum Light Magic Power of a Hero is 1.4B, and the maximum Light Magic Power of a Troops March is 4.2B.

    See Light Magic Power.
