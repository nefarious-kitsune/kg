    • Witch's Lab unlocks about 30–36 days after server opening. From Witch's Lab, Lords can get two Base Powers: Light Magic Power and Dark Magic Power. Light Magic Power can be enhanced by level upgrading with Light Reagents. Dark Magic Power can be enhanced by acquiring Dark Reagents through PvP battles.

    Light/Dark Magic Power can be extended with Magic Stones, Summon Altar, etc.

    Light and Dark Magic

    Light and Dark Magic do not need to be balanced.

    To enhance Light Magic Power, the Lord consumes Light Reagent to upgrade Light Magic level.

    To enhance Dark Magic Power, the Lord need to obtain Dark Reagent through PvP battles. Dark Magic level upgrade is automatic
