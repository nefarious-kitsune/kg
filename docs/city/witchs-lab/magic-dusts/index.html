    • Magic Dusts

    Constructing a Magic Gem at the Witch's Lab requires 100 Magic Dusts. The Witch's Lab can be equipped with maximum 6 Magic Gems at each tier level.

    Note that Magic Dusts cannot be merged to create a higher-tier Magic Dusts.

    Sales and Events

    Magic Dusts can be obtained through sales, Mightiest Kingdom events, and Alliance Treasure events:

    • Pre-Season to Season 11
    • Season 12 to Season 24
    • Season 25 to Season 37
    • Season 38 to Season 46
