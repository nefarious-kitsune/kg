    Dragon

    In the Tower Defense mode, the last defense is initially a defenseless Dragon Egg. After the necessary Dragon Crystals are collected from Dragon Quest chapters, the Dragon would hatch and appears in its baby form. The form will gradually change to the adult form as the Dragon is upgraded.

    In the Map mode, the hatched Dragon is part of every Troop March. The Dragon provides Dragon Base Power to each Troop March.

    Dragon Base Power depend on the levels of Emblems (Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Legendary). Each Emblem's level can be enhanced by consuming Dragon Runes.

    Dragonden

    Dragonden, one of many City structures, unlocks when the Dragon is hatched. The structure can be used for upgrading Dragon, crafting Dragon Gears (Armors), and collecting Dragon King's Gifts (Dragon Rune or Gem).

    Dragon Gears

    Crafting a Dragon Gear (Armor) requires 100 Blueprints. Each Dragon Gear provides Dragon Gear Power that is a a percentage of the Dragon Base Power. The Dragon can be equipped with maximum 8 Dragon Gears.
