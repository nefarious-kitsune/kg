    • Magic Books

    Lords can summon a Summon Monster with 500 Magic Books.

    Note that Magic Books cannot be merged to create a higher-tier Magic Book.

    Dimension Tunnel

    Magic Books can be obtained through Dimension Tunnel Shop:

    • Tier-1Treant Guard: after Sentry War
    • Tier-2Armored Shark: Season 2
    • Tier-3Rainbow Pony: Season 4
    • Tier-4Immortal Phoenix : Season 10
    • Tier-5Imperial Sword : Season 16

    Sales and Events

    The Summon Monster requires 500 Magic Books. Lords need to finish top 3 in Mightiest Kingdom events at least 3 times to complete a Summon Monster.

    Magic Books can be obtained through sales, Mightiest Kingdom events, and Alliance Treasure events:

    • Pre-Season to Season 15
    • Season 16 to Season 30
    • Season 31 to Season 45
