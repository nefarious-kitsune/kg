    • Forge Blueprints

    Constructing a Hero Gear at the Blacksmith requires 100 Forge Blueprints. Each Hero can be equipped with maximum 1 Hero Gear.

    Note that Forge Blueprints cannot be merged to create a higher-tier Forge Blueprints.

    Sales and Events

    Forge Blueprints can be obtained through sales, Mightiest Kingdom events, and Alliance Treasure events:

    • Pre-Season to Season 11
    • Season 12 to Season 24
    • Season 25 to Season 37
    • Season 38 to Season 46
