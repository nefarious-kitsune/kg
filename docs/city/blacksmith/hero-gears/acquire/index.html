    • Acquiring Hero Gears

    A Hero Gear (Weapon) can be crafted from 100 Forge Blueprints of the same tier.

    Note that Forge Blueprints cannot be merged to create a higher-tier Forge Blueprints.

    Hero Gears can be acquired through sales, Mightiest Kingdom, Burning Expedition, Discount Pass, and Alliance Treasure:
