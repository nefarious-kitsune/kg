    • Hero Gears (Weapons) can be acquired from crafting or purchasing.

    Crafting

    T1 Hero Gears can be crafted for free.

    T2 or Hero Gears can be crafted from Forge Blueprints from event rewards:

    • Mightiest Kingdom phase and ranking rewards
    • Burning Expedition Personal Ranking rewards

    Each T2 or above Hero Gear requires 100 Forge Blueprints of the same tier.

    Each Hero Gear requires 100 Forge Blueprints of the same tier.

    Purchasing

    T2 or above Hero Gears can be purchased directly. The prices may vary with seasons:

    • Pre-Season to Season 11
    • Season 12 to Season 24
    • Season 25 to Season 37
    • Season 38 to Season 46
    • Download hero-gear-prices.tsv

    T2 or above Hero Gears can be purchased indirectly via:

    • Alliance Treasure events
    • Discount Store Pass
