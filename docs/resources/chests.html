Chests

Information on Chests.

Normal Chests

Each chest contains one (1) type of treasure.

Treasure Quantity Probability
Normal Recruitment Card 1 21%
Skill Shards 1 8%
50k Gold 1 29%
Rare Dragon Rune 1 22%
T1 Unit EXP Book 1 21%

Normal Chests can be acquired from:

  • VIP Shop
  • Join Rally event
  • Alliance Quests
  • Jennifer's Station
  • Lava Trial

Advanced Chests

Each chest contains five (5) types of treasures of fixed quantities. Each chest has about 10% probability of containing a T3 Armor Blueprint

Treasure Quantity
100k Gold 1
Magic Stone 10
Restore: 1h 1
Stone 10
T1 Unit EXP Book 2
T3 Armor Blueprint 1

Normal Chests can be acquired from:

  • Rally on Titans and Evil Guardians

Supreme Chest

Each chest contains six (6) types of treasures of varying quantities.

Treasure Quantity
100k Gold ~3
Stone ~60
Excellent Dragon Rune ~8
Skill Shard ~3
Daniel ~60
T1 Unit EXP Book ~29

Supreme Chests can be acquired from:

  • Evil Spirit Invasion ranking rewards
