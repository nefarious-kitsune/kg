Hero Collection

If your server is in Pre-Season Phase (it's under the Great Fog or just got out of the Fog), focus on collecting heroes. You can earn gems by doing Bounty Hall quests, but the number of quests and the success rates depend on your hero count.

Heroes to collect via Bounty Hall

  • Sahar, O'Neil, Ariza
  • Cosette, Paul, Wallis, Dolvar
  • Jessica, Rudolph, Ralph
  • Gruen, Meniere, Issac

Note: focus on getting Jessica to 2-star first. You will need as many AP-discount heroes as you can get when your server enters Burning Expedition in Seasonal Phase

Heroes to collect via Lucky Wheel

Heroes that you can obtain via Lucky Wheel. Some of these heroes are also available via Advanced Recruitment cards.

  • Gabriel, Arthur, Jennifer
  • Erika, Allen, Gro
  • Paula, Hadi, Ralph
  • Rosamond, Catherine

Note: you cannot get Rosamond from Free-Pick cards until your server enters Expedition Season 8, so Rosamond should be a priority if you need a Goblin March.

Heroes to collect via Free Picks

  • Pan
  • Nicole

Heroes to collect via Special Events

When there is a main special event where you can collect new heroes, use the event to collect many new heroes, especially Ice Wizard heroes. You need many Ice Wizard heroes to increase success rate and crit rate in Bounty Hall.
