
## What is Hero Tier and Why You Should ignore it?

Make no mistake. If you are very new to this game and don't know how this game work, then Hero Tier is a good starting point to help you understand if a hero is good.

And if you have __no spending limit__ and __will get all available heroes to 5-stars__ anyway, then Hero Tier is useful because all you're concerned is deciding what hero you should use. In that case, stop reading.

That said, most players are either F2P or light spenders, and the main concern is how to spend money and resources most *efficiently* and *effectively*. In that case, chasing S-tier heroes is usually *not* the best thing they can do.

**What's the problem with Hero Tier?**

Firstly, Hero Tier is a rank assuming __all heroes are fully promoted__ (5-star). That's a luxury that most players do not have. In many cases, players are choosing among various SSR and SR heroes at 2 ~ 4 star level. And heroes at these levels are definitely not equivalent to fully promoted heroes.

Secondly, Hero Tier usually only consider the hero's performance as a [warrior hero](../heroes/hero-roles) (the lead hero in a march during a PvP battle). That's not the only role a hero can have. A good team of troops marches will consist of heroes that fulfill a mix of various roles.
