Rally is a common way of teaming up. Attacks launched in rallies come with greater power.

Shared Bonus Power

Troops of players taking part in rallies will receive some Shared Power Bonus.

Shared Power Bonus includes

  • Hero Bond bonus power
  • Unit Talent bonus power
  • Dragon Gear bonus power
  • Magic Stone bonus power
  • Summon Altar bonus power
  • Alliance bonus power
  • Tech bonus power
  • VIP bonus power
  • Skin bonus power

Shared Skills

Troops of players taking part in rallies will receive some Shared Wound Regeneration Bonus.

Wound Regeneration Bonus may come from

  • Regeneration hero skill
  • Broken Land tech
  • Monster Counter hero skill (Titan rally only)

Rally

When calculating rally troops' power, the bonus rate will be compared with that of the rally captain and the higher rate will be taken

Castle Stationing

When a rally troop is stationed at a player's castle, the rally captain will be regarded as the player who reinforces the castle by default. The Power Bonus rate of the captain's first troop will be compared with those of other players and the higher rate will be taken.

War Building Stationing

When stationing at Throne War Buildings, the Battle of the Dragon Buildings, or the Broken Land Buildings, the rate of the player with the higher Power/Skill Bonus among all stationed players will be selected to calculate the overall bonus of all stationing troops.
