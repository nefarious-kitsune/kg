Passing Tower Defense Stages

Some tips on passing tower defense levels.

  • When you go to bed, load the game and keep your phone plugged in to the charger. The game may crash and shut down at some point, but you should have passed a few dozen stages by the time you wake up.

  • Use the proper Tower Defense layout

    • Version 2 layout
    • Version 3 layout

  • If you are stuck:

    • keep training new units and merge them until all units are at max level.
    • Upgrade your heroes
    • Upgrade your units in the Barrack.
