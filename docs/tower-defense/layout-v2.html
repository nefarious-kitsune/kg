Tower Defense Layout (v2)

The version 2 layout is available on most servers.

  • There are 7 Units towers for either troops units or heroes.
  • Heroes in tower defense are the same as lead heroes used in World Map, and they can only be switched when the troops marches are changed.

Tips

  • On Tower 1, use Ice Wizard or a hero.

  • On Tower 2 and 5, use melee heroes.

  • On Tower 3 and 7, use Goblins. When Frost Cyclops approach tower 3 and 7, Frost Cyclops will pause to freeze the tower units; the pauses let your other units deal more damages.

  • On Tower 4 and 6, use range heroes or other units.
