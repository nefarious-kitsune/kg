Tower Defense Units

Heroes

Melee heroes (heroes carrying swords, clubs, or other physical weapons) can deal short-range AoE attacks. They should be placed at U-turns so that they can deal more damage with extra time.

Range heroes (heroes carrying bows, magic staff, daggers, or other long range weapons) can deal long-rang single attacks. They should be placed far back.

Units

Archer can deal single attacks, and every attack is a crit attack (double damage). Archer is best used against large invaders.

Fire Mage can deal AoE attacks (circle-shape) with some chance of critical AoE attacks (double damage to all units in range). Fire Mage is best used against a crowd of small invaders.

Ice Wizard can deal AoE attacks (line-shape) and can slow down some invader units. Fire Mage is best used in conjunction with a melee hero.

Goblin can deal single attacks, and can release poison dealing damage to all invader units. Goblin is best used in the beginning or middle section to increase their skill trigger rate.
