    • Magic Books

    Summoning a Monster at the Summon Altar requires 500 Magic Books. The Summon Altar can be equipped with maximum 1 Monster.

    Note that Magic Books cannot be merged to create a higher-tier Magic Book.

    Dimension Tunnel

    Magic Books can be obtained through Dimension Tunnel Shop:

    • T1 (Treant Guard): after Sentry War
    • T2 (Armored Shark): Season 2
    • T3 (Rainbow Pony): Season 4
    • T4 (Immortal Phoenix): Season 10
    • T5 (Imperial Sword): Season 16

    Sales and Events

    Summoning a Summon Altar Monster requires 500 Magic Books, and collecting the necessary quantity would require finishing top 3 in Mightiest Kingdom at least 3 times.

    Magic Books can be obtained through sales, Mightiest Kingdom events, and Alliance Treasure events:

    • Pre-Season to Season 11
    • Season 12 to Season 24
    • Season 25 to Season 37
    • Season 38 to Season 46
