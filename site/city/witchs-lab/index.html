    • Witch's Lab unlocks about 30–36 days after server opening. The building provides Light Magic and Dark Magic. Light Magic can be enhanced by consuming Light Reagent. Dark Magic can be enhanced through PvP challenges.

    Light and Dark Magic do not need to be balanced.

    Light Magic

    The Light Magic Level is enhanced by consuming Light Reagent. For every Light Reagent consumed, the Light Magic Power increases by 1400. The maximum Light Magic Level is 2000 and requires 1000000 Light Regent.

    See Light Magic

    Dark Magic

    Dark Magic Power can be enhanced by obtaining Dark Reagent through PvP battles. Dark Magic level upgrade is automatic

    Magic Stones

    Light Magic and Dark Magic Powers can be enhanced with Magic Stones. Each Magic Stone requires 100 Magic Dust
