    • On the battlefield, there are 4 Elemental Titans at corners.

    Skills

    Each Elemental Titan is equipped with a shield and can only be damaged by troops of a specific counter element. The shield can be temporarily removed by Counter Holy Sword.

    Titan Location Counter Element
    Titan of Earth NE
    Titan of Fire SE
    Titan of Ice SW
    Titan of Poison NW
