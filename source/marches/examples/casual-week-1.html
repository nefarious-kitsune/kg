Example build for a casual P2P player after 1 week of playing. — by Kitsune21

March 1

Miku Samar

Hunter

For starting Titan rallies. Miku provides 20% Action-Point discount, so she can help do more monster rallies and Jennifer's Station

March 2

Kris Ophelia

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Kris has bonus March Speed and Gathering Speed. These three heroes get more exp-level upgrades so that the Troops March has more carrying load and can stay in a Dragon Nest longer.

March 3

Sabastian Harold

Builder

For building obelisks. Sabastian has bonus March Speed so the March can spend less time traveling.

March 4

Anko Rosamond

Builder

For building obelisks. Anko has bonus March Speed so the March can spend less time traveling.

March 5

Claudia Alucard

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Claudia can increase offline gold output.
