Example build for a casual P2P player after 3 weeks of playing. — by Kitsune21

March 1

Miku Samar

Hunter

For starting Titan rallies. Miku provides 20% Action-Point discount, so she can help do more monster rallies and Jennifer's Station

March 2

Kris Erika Ophelia

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Kris has bonus March Speed and Gathering Speed. These three heroes get more exp-level upgrades so that the Troops March has more carrying load and can stay in a Dragon Nest longer.

Erika Kris Ophelia

Warrior

For PvP battles. Erika has bonus March Speed and Recovery Speed. Kris and Ophelia provides bonus Unit Power at just 1-star.

March 3

Pedra Trishy Merlin

Hunter

For starting Titan and Lava Haka rallies. Pedra has bonus March Speed and can recover damage from Lava Haka rallies. Merlin is a filler here.

Trishy Pedra Merlin

Warrior

For PvP battles. Trishy has bonus March Speed and Recovery Speed.

March 4

Dylan Harold

Builder

For building obelisks. Dylan has bonus March Speed.

March 5

Alucard Rosamond

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Alucard can increase offline gold output.
