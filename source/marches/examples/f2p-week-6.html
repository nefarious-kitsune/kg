Example build for a F2P player after 4 weeks of playing. — by Kitsune21

March 1

Kris Ophelia Samar

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Kris has bonus March Speed and Gathering Speed.

March 2

Harold Arthur Arwyn

Hunter

Stage Master

For starting Titan rallies. Harold provides Action-Point discount, so he can help do more monster rallies. Harold can also increase attacks of all units in Tower Defense.

Arthur Harold Arwyn

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Arthur can increase offline gold output. Arthur is obtained through Bounty Hall and Lucky Wheel.

March 3

Jessica Nicole Merlin

Hunter

For starting Titan and Lava Haka rallies. Jessica has bonus March Speed, Action-Point discount, and can recover damage from Lava Haka rallies. Jessica is obtained through Bounty Hall.

Nicole Jessica Merlin

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Nicole can increase offline gold output. Nicole is obtained through Wishing Crystal Ball and Free-Pick Hero Cards.
