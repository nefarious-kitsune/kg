Example build for a casual P2P player after 7 weeks of playing. — by Kitsune21

March 1

Nicole Pedra Trishy

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Nicole can increase offline gold output.

Pedra Trishy Nicole

Hunter

For starting Titan and Lava Haka rallies. Pedra has bonus March Speed and can recover damage from Lava Haka rallies.

Trishy Nicole Pedra

Warrior

For PvP battles. Trishy has bonus March Speed and Recovery Speed.

March 2

Paula Jessica Merlin

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Paula can increase offline gold output.

Jessica Merlin Paula

Hunter

For starting Titan and Lava Haka rallies. Jessica has bonus March Speed and can recover damage from Lava Haka rallies.

March 3

Kris Miku Ophelia

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Kris has bonus March Speed and Gathering Speed. These three heroes get more exp-level upgrades so that the Troops March has more carrying load and can stay in a Dragon Nest longer.

Miku Ophelia Kris

Hunter

For starting Titan rallies. Miku provides 20% Action-Point discount, so she can help do more monster rallies and Jennifer's Station

Erika Ophelia Kris

Warrior

For PvP battles. Erika has bonus March Speed and Recovery Speed.

March 4

Catherine Cosette Samar

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Catherine can increase offline gold output. Note that this Troops March has heroes of different elements. The long-term goal is to have a Goblin March but that's difficult to build in the beginning of the game.

Anko Cosette Samar

Warrior

For PvP battles. Anko has bonus March Speed and Recovery Speed. The long-term goal is to have just one Fire March, so this Troops March will eventually merge with the previous one.

March 5

Arthur Dylan Sahar

Gold Master

For gold gathering. Arthur can increase offline gold output.

Harold Dylan Sahar

Stage Master

For passing stages. Harold can increase attacks of all units in Tower Defense.

Dylan Sahar Arthur

Warrior

For PvP battles. Dylan has bonus March Speed and Recovery Speed. While Sahar is a better hero overall, she needs to be at 4-star to have the Recovery Speed.
