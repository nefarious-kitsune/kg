Before the second phase and third phase, there is a transition phase where the server will have some cross-server events.

See Transition Phase Calendar

What to Expect?

  • Forge Hammer, Light Reagent, and Strengthening Potion available in Dimensional Tunnel store
  • Unit Talent Tier-5 unlocks
  • Some heroes added to Free-Pick Cards and Wishing Crystal Ball
  • Players and Alliances can transfer to other servers.
  • Lava Haka is more difficult. Haka Behemoths are stronger and drop Free-Pick Hero Cards.
  • Dimensional Tunnel leaderboard becomes cross-server.
  • Alliances get both in-server and cross-server ranking rewards for Lava Haka, Evil Spirit Invasion, and Lava Cave.

Cross-Server Events

In the transition phase and phase 3, most events are cross-server.

  • Cross-Server Invasion on every other Sunday. In this event, three servers are matched against each other, and servers
  • Special Events for Avatar Frames, Castle Skins, and/or Heroes.
  • Mightiest Kingdom every week.
  • Ultimate Power every other week.
  • Ultimate Hunting every other week.
  • Alliances can register for Burning Expedition and Battle of the Dragon. The two events happen in Phase 3 Seasons.
  • Cross-Server Auction House happens once every two weeks. Players bid against players in other servers for Padme, Tracy, Clarence, and Tumnus.
