The second phase of the server follows the end of the Sentry War and ends with the Throne War.

See Phase 2 Calendar

What to Expect?

Before Throne War:

  • Blood of Titan available in Dimensional Tunnel store
  • Elemental Vial available in Dimensional Tunnel store

After Throne War ends:

  • R5 of the alliance that captured the Throne can appoint the server's first King or Queen and the King's Hall (or Queen's Hall) opens.
  • Exchange Shop opens
  • Tier 1 Magic Book, Perfect Summoning Spell, and Advanced Summoning Spell available in Dimensional Tunnel store

Welcome Events

In the first phase, the server will have several welcome events. The welcome events do not repeat.

  • Brave the Dragon's Den on Day 27 ~ 34. Players can obtain Avatar Frame, Castle Skin, and Pythia through this event.
  • Second Personal Championship on Day 28 ~ 34. Players can obtain Simon through this event.
  • Second King of Power on Day 31 ~ 34. Players can obtain time-limited Castle Skin through this event.
  • Second in-server Auction House on Day 37 ~ 40. Players can bid against other players in the server for Padme, Tracy, Clarence, and Tumnus.
