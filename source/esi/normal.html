Information on Chests.

Amateur

Starting the event from a Haunted House very close to the Fortress (100km) is a common mistake. The level 10 Boss would hit the Fortress before members are done with normal spirits, so they would miss out on the opportunity for additional points.

Easy

Starting the event from a Haunted House 300km away is recommended for a new alliance. All members would have plenty of time to reinforce the Fortress for additional points.

Intermediate

Starting the event from a Haunted House 400km away would let members have time to reinforce castles targeted by level 14 and 17 Elite Spirits for more points.

Advanced

Starting the event from a Haunted House 800km away would let members have time to reinforce castles targeted by level 7, 14, and 17 Elite Spirits for even more points.
