    • Use

    Forge Hammers can be used to upgrade Blacksmith Tech.

    Acquire

    Forge Hammers can be acquired in various ways:

    • Dimensional Store
    • Broken Land Store
    • Bounty Hall
    • Mightiest Kingdom phase rewards
    • Jennifer's Station
    • Discount Store
    • Lucky Wheel

    Exchange

    When Blacksmith Tech is at the maximum level (2000), Forge Hammers can be exchanged for Elemental Vial at the Exchange Shop. The exchange rate depends on season:

    ×1 Forge Hammer » ×1117 Elemental Vial Season 39
