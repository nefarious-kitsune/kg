Central and Expedition Fortresses are the starting points of Alliance Territory.

  • Obelisks must be connected to either Central Fortress or Expedition Fortress to be valid.
  • Central Fortress and Expedition Fortress do not have to be connected.

Requirements

  • Central Fortress: Alliance has 20 members and at least 1M power
  • Expedition Fortress: Alliance has 60 members and at least 1B power

Construction Time

Construction time for a Fortress depends on the number of building marches. Each Alliance Member can send only 1 Troop March to a Fortress.
