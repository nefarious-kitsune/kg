In Kingdom Guard, servers are matched against each other by Main Cluster, Sub Cluster, or Season.

Main Cluster-Based

The following are based on Main Clusters

  • Dimensional Tunnel
  • Dimensional Tunnel Cross-Server Leaderboard
  • Lave Cave Leaderboard
  • Evil Spirit Invasion Leaderboard
  • Ultimate Power Leaderboard
  • Ultimate Hunting Leaderboard
  • Wishing Pool in special events
  • Cross-Server Leaderboard in special events

Sub Cluster-Based

The following are based on Sub Clusters

  • Mightiest Kingdom
  • Server transfer
  • Alliance Cross-Server Summon

Season-Based

The following are based on Season

  • Cross-Server Invasion matching
  • Burning Expedition matching

Mixed Matching

Servers in X351 (1578 ~ 1598) and C401 (1599 ~ 1691) have the same Season number, so they can be matched against each other even though they have different system configurations.

In Burning Expedition, servers in Season 1 (no prior Burning Expedition experience) are sometimes matched against servers in Season 2.

During server transfer, movements between servers in Season 0 (no prior Burning Expedition experience) and servers in Season 1 are possible.
