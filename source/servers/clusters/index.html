In order to capture the complexity of the actual game mechanism, this site use a grouping scheme that is different from the official scheme.

Servers are grouped into several Main Clusters, Servers in different Main Clusters have different configurations.

Main Clusters are further divided into Sub Clusters according to Season. Main Cluster, Sub-Cluster, and Season can affect cross-server matching.
