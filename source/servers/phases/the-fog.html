  • The Great Fog

{{TITLE}}

In the beginning, there is a Great Fog that divides players in different zones. Players cannot move outside of their zone or join alliances in a different zone. The Fog will gradually lift and allow for movement.

  • On Day 1~6, the map is divided into 8 different zones. (Map)
  • On Day 7, the Fog will partially lift and the 8 zones will merge into 4 zones. (Map)
  • On Day 11, the Fog will partially lift and the 4 zones will merge into 2 zones. (Map)
  • On Day 16, the Fog will completely lift and the whole map will be revealed.
