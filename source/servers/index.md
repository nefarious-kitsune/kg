<h2>Phases</h2>

<p>Each server goes through several phases:
  <a href="../seasons/#pre-season">Pre-Season Phase</a>,
  <a href="../seasons/#transition">Transition Phase</a> (SvS),
  and finally <a href="../seasons/#seasonal">Phase 3</a> (KvK).</p>
</section>

<section>
<h2>Grouping and Matching</h2>

<p>Servers may be divided into <a href="./groups/">Main Groups</a>
  based on certain <a href="./config">configurations</a> that do not change
  over time.</p>

<p>Servers may alternatively be divided into Seasons
  based on certain <a href="./config">configurations</a> that will change
  over time.</p>

<p>Group and Season affect how servers are
  <a href="./matching">matched</a> in cross-server events.</p>
</section>
