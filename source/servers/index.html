Phases

Each server goes through several phases: the Sentry War Phase (Phase 1), the throne War Phase (Phase 2), a transition phase, and finally the KvK Phase (Phase 3).

Grouping and Matching

Servers may be divided into Main Groups based on certain configurations that do not change over time.

Servers may alternatively be divided into Seasons based on certain configurations that will change over time.

Group and Season affect how servers are matched in cross-server events.
