Tips

Alliance Transfer is not always the best option. After an Alliance Transfer, you must rebuild the territory, and many things will reset. If the main alliance in the destination has room and agree to take all your members, asking your members to transfer as individuals may be the better option.

Transfer Process

  • Only the R5 can initiate the Alliance Transfer process. Once Alliance Transfer is initiated, no new member may enter the alliance, but members can leave the alliance.
  • Each member will receive a Transfer Ticket (upper left of the screen) and has 48 hours to respond to the tickets.
    • Accept response means the member will transfer with the alliance.
    • Decline response means the member will leave the alliance.
    • No response within 48 hours means the member will leave the alliance.
  • Alliance Transfer does not need approval from members. Members' response to the Transfer Ticket only indicate members' willingness to transfer.

Resets

Certain progresses get reset after transfer.

Alliance Resets
Alliances Quests Alliances Quests (personal and alliance progresses) will reset. Remind all members to collect all rewards before transfer.
Lava Trial Lava Trial will reset. Before transfer, summon all available Haka Behemoth and Lava Haka.
Evil Spirit Invasion Evil Spirit Invasion resets and the alliance needs to start from level 1 after transfer.
Lava Cave Lava Cave resets, and members will not be able to participate in Lava Cave for the remainder of the week. Remind all members to use all move/attack points and collect all rewards before transfer.
Alliance Offline Resources Offline resources (Gold and Stone) do not reset.
Alliance Tech Alliance Tech do not reset.
