Tips

Important things to remember before/after you transfer.

  • Claim alliance gold before you leave your alliance or transfer.
  • Dimensional Tunnel resets after transfer (your troops automatically get removed from Dimensional Tunnel mines). Send your troops back to the mines after transfer as soon as possible.

Resets

Certain progresses get reset after transfer.

Dimensional Tunnel Your troops automatically get removed from Dimensional Tunnel mines after you transfer. Send your troops back to the mines as soon as possible.
Alliances Quests Complete as few Alliance Quests as possible. If you have any quest completed, claim the rewards before transfer; if you do not, the quests will be marked as completed but you will not get any rewards from it.
Kingdom Chronicle Kingdom Chronicle resets. Claim all Kingdom Chronicle rewards before you transfer.
Lava Trial Rewards limits on Lava Trial reset. Before you transfer, use all available Haka Behemoth and join as many Haka Behemoth rallies as you can.
Wishing Crystal Ball Wishing Crystal Ball resets. Get your free draw first before you transfer. Do max draws if you have lots of gem.
Join Rally Join Rally event resets. Complete the event if you can, and collect all rewards before you transfer.
Lava Cave Lava Cave resets. Use all your points and collect all rewards before you transfer; you will not be able to participate in Lava Cave for the remainder of the week.
Community Mail Community Mail will be cleared after you transfer. Collect all rewards from Community Mail before you transfer.
Friends List Friends List does not reset. If there are people you want to be in touch with after you transfer, add them to your friend list before you transfer. All your prior direct messages will be cleared, but you can send each other messages after you transfer.
Mightiest Kingdom Mightiest Kingdom resets. So do some light upgrades and collect the daily rewards before you transfer. You can collect daily rewards again after you transfer.
Ultimate Hunting Your cross-server points do not reset, but your server points reset.
King/Queen's Hall Purchase limits in King/Queen's Hall reset.
VIP Store Purchase limits in VIP Store reset do not reset.
Discount Store Pass Progress on Discount Store Pass does not reset.
Daily Quests Progress on Daily Quests and weekly points do not reset.
