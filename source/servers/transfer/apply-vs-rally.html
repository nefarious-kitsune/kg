Differences between Transfer Application and Cross-Server Rally.

Differences

Transfer Application Cross-Server Rally
Requirements Lv. 100 castle. Lv. 500 castle and minimum power requirement (if there is any).
Application Can be submitted at any time. Can be submitted during the Cross-Server Alliance Summon event.
Approval 48-hour waiting period and 60 votes are required. No waiting period and acceptance by the R5 is required.
Transfer Not automatic and not compulsory. Transfer is immediate and automatic.
