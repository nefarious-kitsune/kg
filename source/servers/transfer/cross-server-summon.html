At the start of each season, some alliances can post a Summon to recruit players from other servers, and players can apply to transfer to the alliance.

Cross-Server Rally Process

Application

A player can respond to a Summon and apply for transfer (Rally). A player can have maximum 3 Rallies (applications) at a time.

Approval

The R5 of the summoning alliance can review the rallies and either approve or deny them.

Transfer

Transfer is automatic and compulsory. When the application is approved, the applicant is immediately transferred to the alliance.
