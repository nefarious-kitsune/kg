Many new players are eager to know what are the best heroes (the S-tiers and the A-tiers).

However, for new players, getting the best heroes may not be ideal. Many heroes won't be available in Free-Pick Hero Cards for a long time. For example, Wendy is available on Season 14 (around 360 days after Season 1), and Lilith is available on Season 26 (around 700 days after first season).

Start with SR (purple) heroes and slowly work on SSR (gold) heroes.

Utility Heroes

Utility heroes can help you grow quicker:

Hero Skills Note
Kris
  • +20% Marching Speed
  • +20% Gold Gathering speed
 Use her to help with obelisk building and gold mining
Harold
  • +10% Tower Defense power
  • -10% Action Point
 Use him to pass stages more quickly and save Action Points.
Claudia (1-star)
  • +20% Offline Gold
Use Claudia as a lead hero for more passive gold.

PvP Leaders

PvP lead heroes are useful for in-server wars (Sentry War, Throne War) and cross-server wars (CSI, BotD, BE).

Hero Skills Note
Erika
  • +25% Marching Speed
  • +15% Recovery speed
 Erika is the most economical PvP Leader you can get; You can get her from Lucky Wheel, Wishing Crystal Ball, and Free-Pick Hero Cards.
Anko
  • +30% Marching Speed
  • +15% Recovery speed
 Anko has better Marching Speed than Erika. Initially you must purchase her, but she will become available in Free-Pick Hero Cards when the server enters Transition Phase, so in the long run she is also quite economical.
Jessica can be obtained from Bounty Hall and probably the first SSR AP hero you can get.

Configurations

For best power, change your march configuration depending on the situation. but that is not necessary. you can mix it up. you are in a crunch and badly need gold.

When building To utilize your heroes, it's good to build a Troop March that can fill multiple roles. For example, you can have Jessica, Filius, and Merlin
