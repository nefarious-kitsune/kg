Many new players are eager to know what are the best heroes (the S-tiers and the A-tiers).

However, for new players, getting the best heroes may not be ideal. Many heroes won't be available in Free-Pick Hero Cards for a long time. For example, Wendy is available on Season 14 (around 360 days after Season 1), and Lilith is available on Season 26 (around 700 days after first season).

Start with SR (purple) heroes and slowly work on SSR (gold) heroes.

Utility Heroes

Some heroes are especially useful in the early development:

  • Kris has March Speed and Gold Gathering bonuses, so she is good for building obelisks and gold mining.
  • Harold saves AP (Action Points) for monster quests and has Tower Defense buff, so he is very useful for monster quests and pushing stages.
  • If you need additional gold, consider Claudia. 1-star Claudia can be purchased for only $0.99 and provides you +20% Offline Gold bonus.
Jessica can be obtained from Bounty Hall and probably the first SSR AP hero you can get.

PvP Heroes

  • Erika is the most economic PvP hero you can get. You can get her from Lucky Wheel, Wishing Crystal Ball, and Free-Pick Hero Cards.
