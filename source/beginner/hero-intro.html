Many new players are eager to know what are the best heroes (the S-tiers and the A-tiers).

However, for new players, getting the best heroes may not be in their best interests. Many heroes won't be available in Free-Pick Hero Cards for a long time. For example, Wendy is available on Season 14 (around 360 days after Season 1), and Lilith is available on Season 26 (around 700 days after first season).
