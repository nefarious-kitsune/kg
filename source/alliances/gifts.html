Lords can earn gem from Alliance Gifts and Alliance Treasures.

Normal Gifts

Alliance can claim Normal Gifts from Titan Rallies by Alliance Members. The daily limit is based on member's rank and Alliance Tech.

Special Gifts

Alliance can claim Special Gifts from purchases by Alliance Members. There is no daily limit.

Treasure Chests

When Alliance Members claim Normal or Special Gifts, the Alliance collect keys which can be used to unlock Alliance Treasure Chests.
