    Leadership Transfer

    The R5 can transfer leadership to any member. When leadership is transferred, the ranks will swap. For example, if the R5 transfer leadership to a R3 member, the former leader will become an R3.

    If the R5 is inactive for extended period of time, the R5 can be removed from leadership via impeachment.

    An R4 can impeach when the R5 is inactive for 3 days or longer. An R1–R3 can impeach when the R5 is inactive for 5 days or longer.
