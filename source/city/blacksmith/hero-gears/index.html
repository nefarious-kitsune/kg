    Crafting each Hero Gear (Weapon) requires 100 Forge Blueprints of the same tier. Each Hero can be equipped with maximum 1 Hero Gear.

    Equipping/Dismantling

    Each Hero can be equipped with maximum 1 Hero Gear which provides bonus power based on Blacksmith Base Power.

    Unused Hero Gear can be dismantled to recover Forge Blueprints, Elemental Vial, and Blood of Titan used.

    • T1 Hero Gears (Hardwood Weapons)
    • T2 Hero Gears (Dark-Iron Weapons)
