<p>Witch's Lab unlocks about 30&ndash;36 days after server opening.
From Witch's Lab, Lords can get two <em>Base Powers</em>:
<a href="/city/witchs-lab/light-magic/">Light Magic</a> Power and
<a href="/city/witchs-lab/dark-magic/">Dark Magic</a> Power.
Light Magic Power can be enhanced by 
<a href="/city/witchs-lab/light-magic/upgrade-one-level">level upgrading</a>
with Light Reagents. Dark Magic Power can be enhanced by acquiring
Dark Reagents through PvP battles.</p>
