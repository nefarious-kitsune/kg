<p>Witch's Lab unlocks about 30&ndash;36 days after server opening.
The building provides
<a href="/city/witchs-lab/light-magic/">Light Magic</a> and
<a href="/city/witchs-lab/dark-magic/">Dark Magic</a>.
Light Magic can be enhanced by consuming Light Reagent.
Dark Magic can be enhanced through PvP challenges.</p>
