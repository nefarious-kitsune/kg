<p>Each Hero in a <a href="/marches/">Troops March</a> has
a Light Magic Power based on the Light Magic Level.
The maximum Light Magic Power of a Hero is
<span class="number">1.4</span><span class="unit">B</span>, and
the maximum Light Magic Power of a Troops March is
<span class="number">4.2</span><span class="unit">B</span>.</p>
