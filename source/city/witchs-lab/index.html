    Light and Dark Magic

    Light and Dark Magic do not need to be balanced.

    To enhance Light Magic Power, the Lord consumes Light Reagent to upgrade Light Magic level.

    To enhance Dark Magic Power, the Lord need to obtain Dark Reagent through PvP battles. Dark Magic level upgrade is automatic, and partial levelling is possible (e.g. it is possible to have Dark Magic Power between level 1900 and 1901).
