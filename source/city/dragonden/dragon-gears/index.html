    Equipping/Dismantling

    The Dragon can be equipped with maximum 8 Dragon Gears which provides Dragon Gear Power that is a percentage of the Dragon Base Power.

    Unused Dragon Gear can be dismantled to recover Forge Blueprints, Dragon Soul Stone, and Deluxe Dragon Soul Stone used.
