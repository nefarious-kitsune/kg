    • Acquiring Dragon Gears

    A Dragon Gear (Armor) can be acquired from events or from purchasing.

    From Events

    Blueprints can be acquired from various events. Crafting a Dragon Gear requires 100 Blueprints of the same tier.

    • From Mightiest Kingdom events
    • From BE Personal Ranking rewards
    • From BE Throne Occupation rewards
    • From Evil Spirit Invasion events

    Via Purchasing

    Dragon Gears can be purchased directly. The prices vary with seasons:

    • Pre-Season to Season 11
    • Season 12 to Season 24
    • Season 25 to Season 37
    • Season 38 to Season 46
    • Download dragon-gear-prices.tsv

    Dragon Gears can be purchased indirectly via:

    • Alliance Treasure events
    • Discount Store Pass
