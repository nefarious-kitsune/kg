When you claim Bounty Hall rewards, you can see the results of your quest.

If the quest fails, you will receive some gems as consolation.
If the quest is successful, you will receive the full rewards.
If the quest is critically successful, you will receive double the rewards.
If the quest received upgrade and is successful, you will receive the double the rewards.
If the quest received upgrade and is critically successful, you will receive triple the rewards.
