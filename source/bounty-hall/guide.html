For beginning players, Bounty Hall is the best way to earn SSR Heroes and other valuable resources. Follow these steps to maximize your resource earning.

Collect Heroes

You need many heroes to increase your quest queue and increase success rate. Follow the Hero Collection guide to collect as many heroes as you can.

Refresh for SSR Quests

If you have collected enough heroes and can have 100% success rate on SSR quests, use gem to refresh quests to get high-value quests (SSR heroes and Dragon Rune). Try not to be too picky on what SSR quests you get.

High-Value Quests

The following quests are typically considered high-value:

  • Jessica
  • Sahar
  • Cosette
  • Paul
  • Gruen
  • Purple and Gold Dragon Runes

If you have a high-value quest:

  • Manually dispatch heroes. Auto-Join is unreliable and sometimes gives you <100% success rate.
  • Upgrade the quest if the success rate is 100%; you can earn double the rewards.

Gem Quest

On G4, G5, and G6 servers, Bounty Hall sometimes has Gem Quests but they are extremely rare. Whenever you see a Gem Quest, always start the quest (even if it is a normal quest).

Do not be greedy and refresh a quest multiple times to get a Gem Quest. The probability of Gem Quest is very low and you would spend more gem than you can earn.
