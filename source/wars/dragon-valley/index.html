Rushing to the Dragon Valley is a tournament event that repeats every 4 weeks.

Matching

In the events, alliances face each other in 1-vs-1 battles in Dragon Valley Showdowns.

Overview

The Alliances start at two opposing corners, and they will expand their territory by claiming Dragon Palaces. The Dragon Palaces are guarded by monsters and their strength vary depending on the power of the participating alliances.

There are nine (9) Dragon Palaces in total. The battle ends when one alliance claims all 9 Palaces.

Players can attack 4 Element Lords (their strength also vary depending on the power of the participating alliances). When players defeat an Element Lord, the alliance gains power. When an Element Lord is defeated, it will resurrect after a 5-minute cooldown.

Tips

  • The event happens alongside Burning Expedition. Players need to teleport back to Normal Land in order to enter the Valley.
  • Alliances should organize participating members into teams, with the bigger players concentrating on Dragon Palaces and the smaller players concentrating on Element Lord.
  • Alliances should focus on elements that their members specialize on first.
  • Use AP-discount heroes (Miku, Jessica, Pan, etc.) when attacking Dragon Palaces. The heroes will recover from all damage from the attacks.
  • According to in-game description, troops do not receive damage from attacking Element Lords.
