Regular Events

In the Seasonal Phase, regular events happen regularly on a 28-day cycle

Cross-Server Alliance Events

Time Event Description
Mon & Thu every week Evil Spirit Invasion Defend against evil spirits and be rewarded with Blueprints, Dragon Soul Stone, Silver, Restore, and Normal/Advanced Chests
Mon–Fri every week Lava Trial Challenge Lava Behemoth and Lava Haka for a chance to get Free-Pick SSR Hero Cards.
Wed every week ES Battlefield Battle monsters and bosses in the Evil Spirit Battlefield for a chance to get Gem, Unit EXP Book, Elemental Vial, Forge Blueprints, and other rewards.
