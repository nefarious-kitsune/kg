Maximizing Points

Stay close to hive
With each attack wave, alliances members only have 5 minutes to reinforce. Stay close to everyone else so reinforcements can be sent quickly and timely.
Stay online and send reinforcement

Check the event notifications. If a castle needs reinforcement, the notification box will be highlighted in red.

If you cannot be online the whole time, set a timer for the Elite and Boss waves:

  • Wave 7: 30 min
  • Wave 10: 45 min
  • Wave 14: 1 hr 5 min
  • Wave 17: 1 hr 20 min
  • Wave 20: 1 hr 35 min
Teleport with caution

If your castle is not being targeted by any Evil Spirit, you may teleport your castle to reinforce others. Your castle may receive a temporary ❌❌ mark, but the mark should reset to the previous state on the next attack wave.

However, if your castle is being targeted (an Evil Spirit is marching toward you) and you teleport, your castle will receive ❌❌ mark and you will no longer get any Evil Spirit.

If your castle has ❌❌ mark…
If your castle has ❌❌ mark, it means you will no longer get any attack from Evil Spirits. So do as much reinforcement as you can.
Do not over-reinforce

If you reinforce a member with a Troop March that is substantially bigger than his/her total power, you may steal points and the member being reinforced could get no point.

When you reinforce others, send a Troop March with appropriate power. Power down your Troop March if necessary.
