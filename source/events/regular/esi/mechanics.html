R5 or R4 of an alliance can choose to start the event from any Haunted House. A total of 20 waves of Evil Spirits will depart from the Haunted House and invade castles and/or Alliance Fortress.

Attack Waves

  • After the event is started, there is an initial countdown of 30 seconds.
  • After the initial countdown, the Haunted House will dispatch one wave of attack every 5 minutes.
  • Each wave will take 1 to 5 minutes to hit the targets.

15 waves of Normal Spirits will attack all members' castles. The Spirits will get stronger with each wave.

Attack Targets

If the event is started on Normal Land:

    • Normal Wave: Normal Evil Spirits will attack all member castles on the map
    • Elite Wave: 10 Elite Evil Spirits will attack randomly selected member castles on the map
    • Boss Wave: Evil Spirit King will attack the Alliance Fortress closest to the starting point

If the event is started on Broken Land:

    • Normal Wave: Normal Evil Spirits will attack all member castles on Broken Land
    • Elite Wave: 10 Elite Evil Spirits will attack randomly selected member castles on Broken Land
    • Boss Wave: Evil Spirit King will attack a randomly selected member castle on Broken Land

Fending Off Attacks

Garrisoned troops will not get battle damage from Evil Spirits.

If a castle fails to fend off a Normal Spirit once, the castle will get an ❌ mark. If the castle fails to fend off a Normal Spirit the second time, the castle will get ❌❌ marks, and Normal Spirits will stop attacking the castle.

If a castle teleports during the event, the castle will get ❌❌ marks, and no Spirit will attack the castle.

Castles do not get an ❌ mark from a failed defense against an Elite Spirit.
