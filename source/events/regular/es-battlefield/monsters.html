In the Evil Spirit Battlefield event, Lords can attack monsters for points and chest rewards.

Searching for Monsters

Monsters are not visible at the start of the event. To see the monsters, Lords need to use the Search Map function. Lords can search for T1 Monster (Orc Warrior) at the start of the event. Lords can search for higher tier monsters at approximately 4 minutes after the start of the event.

Monsters and Chests

When the Lord defeats a monster on the Evil Spirit Battlefield, the Lord can obtain an Evil Spirit Chest. The Lord needs to return the chest to the castle to get rewards.

Notes on Chests

  • Carrying an Evil Spirit Chest will increase the Troop March's movement speed.
  • Rewards will only be settled when the Evil Spirit Chest is brought back to the castle.
