On the ES Battlefield, Lords can attack monsters for points and rewards.

Monsters

Monsters are not visible at the start of the Evil Spirit Battlefield event. To see the monsters, Lords need to use the Search Map button.

When the Lord defeats a monster on the ES Battlefield, the Lord can obtain an Evil Spirit Chest. The Lord needs to return the chest to the castle to get rewards.
