Schedule

  • On day 1, day 12, and day 21, no Goddess is present.
  • On day 2–4, Goddess of Harvest is present.
  • On other days, a randomly selected Goddess is present.
Goddess of Light
  • Lords can heal all wounded units in the castle for free
  • Max 5 free heals (10 with Crystal Season Card)
Goddess of Earth
  • Lords can teleport the cities for free within the Broken Land.
  • Max 5 free teleport (10 with Crystal Season Card)
Goddess of War
  • Each Faction can immediately occupy the the first 10 buildings without any building time.
  • +5% Proportion of Slightly Wounded (+10% with Crystal Season Card)
Goddess of Life
  • Lords can recover power of wounded Troop March with Crystal.
  • Lords with Crystal Season Card can earn double Expedition Gold with Crystal used for recovering wounded units.
Goddess of Fairness
  • The number of times a Lord can attack castle is limited.
  • Max 50 castle attacks (100 with Crystal Season Card)
Goddess of Harvest
  • Lords can collect Harvest Chest (1 Chest per 3000K gold gathered)
  • Each Harvest Chest may contain Emblem Chest (probability unknown)
  • Max 5 Harvest Chests each day (max 10 with Crystal Season Card)
Goddess of Darkness
  • Lords can upgrade Dark Magic level beyond level 2000.
  • Max 10 additional levels each season.
