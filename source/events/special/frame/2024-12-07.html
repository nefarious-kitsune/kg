Event Sections

Sound Wave Collection

Collect Note Cards: During the event, completing quests can earn additional event rewards. The daily reward quantity for each type of quest is limited to refresh at everyday UTC 00:00. (Screenshot)

Colorful Night of Music

Consume Note Cards: The player use collected Note Cards to draw poker cards and play Blackjack. If the total card points is > 21, the hand is considered a bust, and no points will be awarded; if exactly = 21, the points will be doubled. (Screenshot)

Music Pass

Pass: Use the points obtained from the Colorful Night of Music to unlock pass rewards. Unlocking advanced rewards will grant you higher quality and more abundant rewards. (Screenshot)

Music Wheel

Consume Instrument: Use instruments to spin the wheel and win rewards. Spinning the wheel will earn points, and once the points accumulate to a certain score, you can claim points rewards. Spin continuously for 10X for an extra surprise discount.

You can switch the wheel mode in the upper right corner of the page; the points earned in Normal mode and Crazy mode are different. (Normal Mode Screenshot, Crazy Mode Screenshot)

Dream Music Exchange

Consume Music Coupons: Use music vouchers to exchange for rewards at the Dream Music Exchange. Each item has a limit on the exchange quantity, collect more music vouchers to exchange for more rewards! (Screenshot)
