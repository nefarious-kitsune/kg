Event Format

Chest Collection

Collect Grain Storage Bags: Search for chest locations and dispatch troops to collect chests. For every 4 chests collected, you can receive cumulative collection rewards. (Screenshot)

Grain Storage Pass

Consume Grain Storage Bags: Grain Storage Bag items obtained from Chest Collection can be used to unlock the rewards of this pass. Unlocking advanced rewards grants higher-quality items. (Screenshot)

Winter Grain Daily Record

Five-Day Login: Log in to the game daily during the event period to participate in quests and receive additional event item rewards. (Screenshot)

Lucky Wheel

Consume Gold Action Points: The treasure vault has two modes: Normal and Crazy. Tap the button in the upper right corner to switch. Continuous draws of 10 times enjoy a discount, and reaching a certain number of points allows you to claim rewards. (Screenshot)

Winter Blessing Pool

Gem Wishing Pool: Consume gems to make a wish, with a chance to win a super grand prize. The first 100 wishes can receive Potion Flask items. (Screenshot)
