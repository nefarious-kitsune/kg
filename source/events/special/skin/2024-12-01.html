Event Format

Snowfield First Aid

Collect Energy Stones by completing daily quests:

  • Defeat Titan and monsters
  • Pass stages
  • Gather gold
Pinball Legend

Consume Energy Stones to play a marble game. The score from each round is based on the points from the box where the marble land. The marbles may also collide with the Barricade during their descent, and after 5 collisions, the Lord can earn extra points displayed by the Barricade.

Road to Rescue

Consume game points earned from the mini-game to unlock Event Pass rewards for Medical Kit:

  • Free-tier: 500 Medical Kit
  • Paid-tier: 1350 Medical Kit and a permanent Avatar Frame
Wheel of Fate

Consume Medical Kit: The treasure vault has Normal and Crazy modes; click the button in the upper right corner to switch. In Crazy mode, you can choose the latest heroes from the glowing Free-Pick Heroes box. Activating the treasure vault 10 times grants you a discount, and accumulating treasure points lets you claim Extra Rewards. (Screenshot)

Wishing Spring

Gem Wishing Pool: After using Gems to Make a wish, you will receive a wish reward. The first 100 wishes made daily will receive an extra event item, and no additional rewards will be given after exceeding this number. (Screenshot)
