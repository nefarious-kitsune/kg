Event Format

Snowfield First Aid

Collect Energy Stones: Completing quests grants additional item rewards. Each quest type has a daily limit that resets at UTC 00:00. (Screenshot)

Pinball Legend

Consume Energy Stones: You can launch the marbles by consuming Energy Stones and clicking any falling button. Your score for this round is based on the points from the box where the marbles land. The marbles may also collide with the Barricade during their descent, and after 5 collisions, you can earn extra points displayed by the Barricade. (Screenshot)

Road to Rescue

Pass: Points earned from the Marble Legendary event can be used to unlock pass rewards, with advanced rewards offering higher quality items. (Screenshot)

Wheel of Fate

Consume Medical Kit: The treasure vault has Normal and Crazy modes; click the button in the upper right corner to switch. In Crazy mode, you can choose the latest heroes from the glowing Free-Pick Heroes box. Activating the treasure vault 10 times grants you a discount, and accumulating treasure points lets you claim Extra Rewards. (Screenshot)

Wishing Spring

Gem Wishing Pool: After using Gems to Make a wish, you will receive a wish reward. The first 100 wishes made daily will receive an extra event item, and no additional rewards will be given after exceeding this number. (Screenshot)
