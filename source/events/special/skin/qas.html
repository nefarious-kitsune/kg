Do the event tokens carry over?

During a Castle-Skin holiday event, Lords can collect three types of tokens:

Token Example Source Use
Lv. 1 Token Collect from complete quests such as defeating Titans and monsters Consume to play a mini game for Lv. 2 Tokens
Lv. 2 Token Stirring Rod Collect from playing a mini game. Consume to unlock Event Pass rewards for Lv. 3 Tokens
Lv. 3 Token Potion Flask Collect from Event Pass, Gem Wishing Pool, and purchases Consume to spin for Hero Cards and other rewards at Lucky Draws

The Tokens can only be used for the specific event. When the event ends, unused Tokens will be converted and delivered to Lords by mail. Each Lv. 2 Token Token converts to 10. Each Lv. 3 Token converts to 10.

How do I win gem from Gem Wishing Pool?

The Gem Wishing Pool will randomly choose a lucky winner after a specific interval, which is 2 minutes or more. The specific time depends on the activity level of the player pool. On a large player pool (such as G2), the interval is generally close to 2 minutes. On a small player pool (such as G1 or G5), the interval could be more than 20 minutes.

If the player pool is active, the general strategy is one of the following:

  • Use a timer and make wishes when the time from last winner is 2 minutes or more.
  • Make wishes when the total gem prize is 100K or more.
