Event Rewards

In this event, Lords have a chance to obtain the following rewards.

  • Viola
  • Spirit Horn Demon Shadow Frame
  • Mysterious Witch Tower Castle
  • Shadowy Magic Tower Castle

Event Format

Soul Collection

Collect Stirring Rod by completing daily quests:

  • Defeat Titan and monsters
  • Pass stages
  • Gather gold
Magic Combo

Consume Stirring Rod to play a tile-matching mini-game. Each game move requires 5 Stirring Rod.

Road of the Dead

Consume game points earned from the mini-game to unlock Event Pass rewards for Potion Flasks:

  • Free-tier: 500 Potion Flasks
  • Paid-tier: 1350 Potion Flasks and a permanent Avatar Frame
Enigmatic Wheel

Consume Potion Flasks (collected from Event Pass, Wishing Pool, and event pack purchases) to draw rewards from the event treasure vault. Tap the upper-right button to switch between the Normal Mode and the Crazy Mode.

Call of the Dead

Consume Gems to make a wish at the Wishing Pool and receive rewards. The first 100 wishes made daily will grant a Potion Flasks.

Half of all gems used will be added to the grand prize, and one lucky Lord will be randomly selected to win the grand prize.
