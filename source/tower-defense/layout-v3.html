The version 3 layout is available on some selected servers.

  • There are 4 Units towers for troops units
  • There are 4 Hero Towers for heroes. Heroes in tower defense are independent of heroes used in World Map, and they can switched at any time.

Tips

  • On tower A and B, use melee heroes. Harold is recommended.

  • On tower C and D, use range heroes.

  • On tower 1 and 2, use Goblin and/or Ice Wizard

  • On tower 3 and 4, use Fire Mage and/or Archer
