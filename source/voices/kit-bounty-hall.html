By Kitsune21 (2024-05-22)

Why Bounty Hall Matters

Bounty Hall is essential to the development of players on new servers. In the beginning, most SSR heroes are locked (they cannot be obtained from Free-Pick Hero cards) and will remain locked for many seasons. Bounty Hall is the only way for players to acquire heroes necessarily to grow. Bounty Hall also provide resources such as Skill Shards and Gallery Puzzles that players need.

Obtaining heroes and valuable resources from Bounty Hall is very slow and very expensive. Players need means to acquire the gem to fund the process, and gem quests is essential to that.

Issues with Gem Quest

There were 2 main issues with Bounty Hall

Envy

Many veteran players in C200 cluster believe that player on new servers are getting unlimited gem for free and they are envious. However, the reality is:

  • Earning Gem from Gem Quests is not a common knowledge among new players.

  • Players need to collect many heroes to unlock all quests, and that takes some time because many heroes are locked.

  • Players need to collect a high number of heroes to have high success rates and crit rates. That is very difficult (again, most heroes are locked), and players need to take unusual measures, such as collecting new heroes in Special Events (while players on old servers are using the events to get new heroes to high levels). Again, this is not a common knowledge.

Abuse

Gem from Gem Quests were meant to help new players get the fund needed to acquire resources so they can be prepared for events that will be very challenging. However, some players were unnecessarily hoarding gem, and this took away the fun from the game and players weren't growing. Also, some players were recklessly spending gem, and this was bad because learning to manage resource properly is essential for long-term development.

What Need to be Done

Developer must be transparent and open about how various features work. It is not enough to adjust things without

  • Disclosing a list of all the rewards contents

  • Disclosing the probability of each reward content

  • Disclosing whether or not the content and probabilities are different among different clusters

Developer must provide proper guides and properly train their CS reps to answer questions not related to purchases. There is a large information divide among new players and that is harmful to retention of new players.

Developer must provide proper notice to important change. Putting a notice in the Bulletin Notice is not enough because the messages have always been duplicates of what players receive by mail and players have learned to ignore it completely.

Developer must provide friendly gaming experience to new players and F2P players. Currently the configuration on new server is very unfriendly to new players:

  • F2P players do not get VIP points and are stuck with 200 AP queue.

  • F2P players are stuck with 2 troops marches for a long time because the unlocking requirement for Academy has been raised too high.

For new players, the pacing of the game is excruciatingly slow and feels like a full-time job. We are out of COVID and people simply don't have the patience for that.

Developer must provide roll back the changes made to new servers. Personally I think Gem Quest is a temporary solution and should be removed when a server reaches Season 2 or 3. But for new servers, Gem Quest is a must because their server configuration is very unfriendly to F2P and small spenders.
