On the Official Kingdom Guard Discord server, there are currently 4 Recruitment Roles (S1, S2, S3, and S4) for recruiting players and finding servers to transfer to.

Getting the Right Role

  1. Go to #Channel and Roles (first channel on the server)

  2. Select the Role that is appropriate for your server

  3. You can now access the appropriate recruitment channel(s)
