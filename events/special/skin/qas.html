Tips for Castle-Skin Special Event

Event Tokens

During a Castle-Skin special event, Lords can collect two types of tokens. The Tokens are specific to the respective event and do not carry over to the next event.

Token Example Source Use
Lv. 1 Token Stirring Rod Collect from completing quests Consume to play a mini game for game points
Lv. 3 Token Potion Flask Collect from Event Pass, Wishing Pool, and event pack purchases Consume to draw rewards from event treasure vault

Playing the Mini Game

Use only the amount of Lv. 1 Token necessary to complete all 50 reward levels of the Event Pass.

Playing the Wishing Pool

The Gem Wishing Pool will randomly choose a lucky winner after a specific interval, which is 2 minutes or more. The specific time depends on the activity level of the player pool. On a large player pool (such as G2), the interval is generally close to 2 minutes. On a small player pool (such as G1 or G5), the interval could be more than 20 minutes.

If the player pool is active, the general strategy is one of the following:

  • Use a timer and make wishes when the time from last winner is 2 minutes or more.
  • Make wishes when the total gem prize is 100K or more.
